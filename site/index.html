How to use it

Three taps. That’s the whole product.

  1. 1

    Talk (or type)

    Open Capture. Tap the mic and speak like you would to a friend. Add a photo or clip if you have one. Then tap Draft now.

  2. 2

    Edit your words

    Open Drafts. Each platform gets its own caption. Tap the text to keep your wording — cut anything that doesn’t sound like you.

  3. 3

    Share from your phone

    Tap Share, pick Instagram / TikTok / …, then tap Open share sheet. In the list, tap that app. Paste the caption if it didn’t come through.

The part people miss

The share sheet is the post button.

Brand Studio does not log into Instagram for you on the free plan. It hands the video or photo to your phone, and copies the caption to your clipboard.

  1. Tap Share on a draft, then pick the apps.
  2. Tap Open share sheet for TikTok (or Instagram, X, …).
  3. In your phone’s list, tap that app — scroll if you don’t see it yet.
  4. Paste the caption in the description. Post as usual.
  5. Back in Brand Studio, tap Next for the next app. Mark posted when you’re done.

TikTok missing from the list? Use Save video, then upload inside the TikTok app. Same caption — we already copied it.
