## Full description (4000 chars max)

Stop typing your content. Say it.

Brand Studio is the voice-first content studio for creators — the publishing surface that doesn't own a social network. Tap the mic, talk through your idea, and get ready-to-post content for X, Facebook, TikTok, Snapchat, Instagram and LinkedIn — written in YOUR voice, tuned to each platform, in seconds.

WHY CREATORS SWITCH

• Voice-first, mobile-first. Capture ideas the moment they hit — on a walk, after the gym, between takes.
• Platform-native, not copy-paste. A punchy X post, a warm Facebook update, a TikTok script, an Instagram caption, a LinkedIn take — all from one spoken idea.
• Your brand. Tell it your goals, projects, voice and style once. Every draft sounds like you.
• Share always works. One-tap Post and auto-schedule unlock on Creator.
• Daily plan + content queue. Film/edit blocks on Daily; queue drafts to go live on Content.
• Growth tips each week. Algorithm-aware suggestions from your own activity.
• Studio plan. Bring your own AI agent, unlock more drafts, and run Brand Studio on your own server.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create your free account
2. Teach it your brand
3. Tap the mic and talk
4. Share, Post, or schedule

PLANS

• Free — 10 drafts/month, Share everywhere
• Creator ($19/mo) — 200 drafts, Post + schedule
• Studio ($49/mo) — 1,000 drafts, agents, self-hosted server

Invite a friend and you both get bonus drafts.

Built for solo creators, fitness creators, builders-in-public, and anyone who talks better than they type.
