**Universal**
> “Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with Brand Studio. I’ll be brief.  
> Creators, small business owners, and MLM distributors freeze when they open five apps — they can talk the idea, they just can’t ship it.  
> We turn one voice note into ready-to-post captions for X, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and more — then Share, Post, or schedule.  
> Free to try. Curious if you’d take a 15-minute Zoom so I can show you live — or meet IRL if that works better?”

**SMB**
> “Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with Brand Studio.  
> Most SMB owners *are* the marketing department — you talk to customers all day, then never post.  
> Brand Studio: speak what you’d say over the counter, get platform-ready posts in your voice, Share free or Post on a schedule.  
> Fifteen minutes on Zoom — I’ll use an example from [their industry: salon / gym / trades / café]. Interested?”

**MLM**
> “Hi [Name], this is [Rep] with Brand Studio.  
> Network marketers win on conversations — then lose the week to writing posts. Teams burn out on generic scripts.  
> You speak a real story once; we turn it into authentic posts across platforms — Share today, schedule the week.  
> Uplines use it for themselves and invite their team. Got 15 minutes for a live mic demo?”

**If they hesitate:**  
> “No deck required first — I’ll share my screen, tap the mic once, and you’ll see six platform drafts. If it’s not useful, we end early.”

**If they ask price:**  
> “Free to start. Creator is $19 a month when you want one-tap Post and scheduling. Studio is $49 if you want agents or your own server — teams sometimes land there. Happy to show Free first.”

**If gatekeeper (SMB):**  
> “Could you point me to the owner or whoever handles Instagram / Facebook for [Business]? I’ll send a 20-second clip and a calendar link.”

**If gatekeeper (MLM):**  
> “Looking for [Name] or a team lead who posts for recruiting / product. I’ll send a short demo they can forward to their downline.”

## Zoom meeting open (first 60 seconds)

1. “Thanks for the time — I’ll show, not pitch.”  
2. Confirm who they are: creator / SMB / MLM — tailor the spoken example.  
3. Open the app → tap mic → speak one sentence about *their* niche  
   - SMB: “Saturday special / why customers trust us”  
   - MLM: “Why I joined / product win this week / invite to Zoom”  
4. Show Drafts (2–3 platforms).  
5. “Share works free. Post + schedule is Creator at $19.”  
6. Ask: “Does this solve a real weekly pain for you — or for your team / staff?”

**Close options**
- User / SMB: “Want a Creator trial / invite link?”  
- MLM upline: “Want a team walkthrough + referral links for your downline?”  
- Partner / investor: “Want the 12-slide deck + a follow-up with numbers?”  
- Agency: “Happy to do a 30-min studio walkthrough for your clients.”

## Objection cheatsheet

| They say | You say |
|----------|---------|
| “We use ChatGPT” | “Chat ends at copy. We end at Share, Post, or a queue — on your phone.” |
| “We use Buffer” | “Buffer schedules what you already wrote. We create what you just said.” |
| “No time” | “Fifteen minutes. One mic tap. If it’s not obvious, we’re done.” |
| “Send a deck” | “I’ll send the deck *and* a 20s video — still want a live slot so you can hear your own voice turn into posts?” |
| “We’re not creators” | “Perfect — SMBs and network marketers are core. If you talk better than you type, you’re the user.” |
| “MLM tools already exist” | “Most are script libraries. We capture *your* voice so it doesn’t sound like everyone else’s funnel.” |
| “My staff won’t use another app” | “It’s a home-screen icon. Speak, Share — no desktop login. Free tier to prove it.” |
| “My downline won’t pay” | “Free to start; Share works. Uplines often gift Creator or use referrals so the team gets bonus drafts.” |
