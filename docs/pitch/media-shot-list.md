## Hero video A — 15–20 seconds (ads / cold outreach)

**Script (voiceover or on-screen text):**

1. **0–3s** — Face or phone: “I talk better than I type.”  
2. **3–8s** — Tap mic → speak one clear idea (e.g. “Three tips for posting when you’re busy”).  
3. **8–14s** — Swipe Drafts: X / Instagram / TikTok captions appear.  
4. **14–18s** — Tap Share (or Post). Text: **Speak on your phone. Post everywhere.**  
5. **18–20s** — URL on screen: `app-bay-nu-40.vercel.app`

**Caption for posting:**  
Speak once. Get posts for every platform. Brand Studio — free to start.
