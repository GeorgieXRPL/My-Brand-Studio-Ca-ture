## Differentiation (say this on the slide)

1. **Capture is the product** — competitors assume the caption already exists.  
2. **Share is a first-class Free path** — zero OAuth friction to get value.  
3. **Brand brain compounds** — not a single chat thread.  
4. **Uber frame** — we don’t own social; we orchestrate distribution.  
5. **Studio self-host** — enterprise-curious creators and agencies who want data on their box.

## Positioning line vs each

- **vs Buffer/Later:** “They schedule what you already wrote. We create what you just said.”  
- **vs ChatGPT:** “Chat ends at copy. We end at Share / Post / queue.”  
- **vs CapCut:** “They finish the video. We finish the caption stack for every network.”
