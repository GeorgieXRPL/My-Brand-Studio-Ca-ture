# Market sizing — Brand Studio

Sources are public industry estimates (2024–2025). Treat ranges as directional for pitch; refresh before a priced round.

---

## Category definition

**Primary category:** AI-assisted social content creation + light scheduling for solo creators, **SMBs**, and **MLM / network marketers**.

**Adjacent:** Social media management (SMM), creator tools, AI writing, local-business marketing, network-marketing enablement.

---

## TAM / SAM / SOM (working model)

### TAM — Creator / social tooling spend

- Global creator economy often cited in the **hundreds of billions** (Goldman Sachs / SignalFire-style estimates vary widely; use **~$250B+** as a ceiling narrative, not a SaaS TAM).  
- More useful SaaS TAM: **social media management + AI content tools** — industry reports commonly place SMM software in the **low tens of billions** growing double-digits (e.g. Grand View / similar “social media management software market” reports ~$15–25B by late decade).  

**Pitch TAM (SaaS):** ~$20B social publishing / management software worldwide.

### SAM — Weekly posters on 2+ platforms (creators + SMB + MLM)

Bottom-up sketch:

| Input | Assumption |
|-------|------------|
| Addressible English-first weekly posters (creators + SMB owners + MLM distributors) | ~25–40M |
| Willing to pay for a phone-first AI studio | 5–8% |
| SAM users | ~1.5–3M |
| ARPU (blended Free→Creator) | ~$8–12 / mo if 15–25% of actives pay |

**SMB wedge:** owner-operated businesses with no marketing hire.  
**MLM wedge:** high cadence + personal brand; uplines as distribution (team invites / referrals).

**SAM revenue (illustrative):** 2M × $10 × 12 ≈ **$240M ARR** theoretical ceiling in English mobile-first niche — we do not claim we capture this; it frames headroom.

### SOM — 24-month target

| Metric | Target |

Conservative SOM keeps the story credible pre-traction.

## Why this wedge wins share

Buffer / Later own **calendar + analytics**. ChatGPT owns **blank-page generation**. Brand Studio owns **speak → brand drafts → ship on phone** — the moment of creation, not the boardroom calendar.
